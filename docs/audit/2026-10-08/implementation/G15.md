# G15: richer performance statistics

Run reports and comparisons now present measured-request p50/p75/p95/p99, mean, minimum, maximum, population standard deviation, interquartile range, and coefficient of variation. These statistics use final successful uncached requests; warmup, failed, cached, invalid, and timed-out observations stay out of that distribution, with eligible samples and missing measurements counted explicitly.

Reports expose retry-inclusive measured-request latency from the first dispatched attempt through final completion, including retry delay and time between resumed sessions. Comparisons expose the same lifecycle summary for measurement and warmup. Throughput is successful final requests divided by the observed phase span from first dispatch start through final completion; concurrent work shares the elapsed span and retries extend it. Missing timestamps or uncommitted dispatches leave the rate unavailable.

Warmup latency and throughput are shown separately from measured work. The output describes the harness phase and does not claim that the application or its remote dependencies are physically cold or warm. Existing comparison field `latency_p95_ms` remains available for regression policy compatibility.

Recovery notes that cannot be matched to a known work item remain phase-neutral. In that case neither warmup nor measurement throughput claims a complete rate, and retry-inclusive phase summaries suppress samples that could omit an unknown earlier dispatch. Markdown and HTML reports identify the unavailable reason; JSON and comparison output expose completeness fields.
